Zearaf Tech — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 19, 2026

Zearaf Tech ("we," "our," or "us") develops and publishes Android mobile applications including Bloomstone: Cozy Garden Match, Last Lighthouse: Ghost Beam, and Plants vs Pests: Lane Defense. This Privacy Policy describes how data is handled when you play our games or visit our website.

1. Information & Third-Party Platform Services

Our games are playable offline without creating a Zearaf Tech account. When connected to the internet, our Android apps may use standard Google platform services:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Games: Used for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, and cloud save synchronization.
  • Google AdMob & Firebase Diagnostics: Used to serve optional rewarded, banner, or interstitial ads and collect non-personal crash/performance diagnostics and Advertising IDs to keep our games free to play.

2. Local Device Storage

Game progress, unlocked stages, upgrades, and audio preferences are stored locally on your Android device. Uninstalling a game may erase local save data unless backed up via Google Play Games cloud save.

3. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. Ad configurations comply with Google Play family and content rating guidelines.

4. Contact

For support or privacy inquiries, please contact us through the developer contact email listed on our official Zearaf Tech Google Play Store pages.
